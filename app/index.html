Five minutes with the demo

  1. As Sonar Apparel (the manufacturer), open the claim. It is held by three gaps, one at each weak point: waste origin, the spinner's packing list, and your own cutting report.
  2. Open handoff T3 in the chain. The packing list shows 3,180 kg against a TC of 3,000 kg. Click Raise gap.
  3. Switch Viewing as to Rahman Jhut Traders. Complete the declaration: collection dates 14–28 Jun 2026, add “Siddhirganj Knitwear, Cutting room, Narayanganj”, declaration number RMD-RJT-0626, tick signed. Gap G1 closes itself.
  4. Switch to Meghna Spinning Mills, open T3, edit the packing list to the net weight, 3,000 kg.
  5. Switch back to Sonar Apparel. Correct P4 to 13,500 pieces (13,500 × 0.19 kg fits in 2,590 kg of fabric). The claim is released.

Data is kept in this browser only. Reset demo restores the starting state.
