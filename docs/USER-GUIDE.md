# User guide and document list

What each company does in Threadback, and every document and figure it supplies. Use the tables in supplier interviews: they are the complete evidence a recycled-cotton claim needs.

## Documents per tier

| Tier | Once | For every batch or shipment it sells | For every production run |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Waste trader** (no certificate) | Company details | Weighbridge slip number (and photo) · number of bags · each source factory with area, kg and collection date · waste type · colour sort · fibre content · confirmed free of elastane and prints · pre- or post-consumer · **signed reclaimed material declaration** (finger signature or photo of the paper form) | — |
| **Recycler** (GRS) | GRS scope certificate | **At goods-in:** own purchase order · trader's invoice or cash memo · weight at own gate and gate slip · moisture % · **countersignature** of the trader's declaration. **When selling fibre:** transaction certificate (TC) · purchase order · invoice · net packing list | Waste used per goods-in (kg) · fibre out (kg) · sorting rejects (kg) · sorting log, shredder output sheet |
| **Spinner** (GRS) | GRS scope certificate | TC with recycled % · purchase order · invoice · **net** packing list (without cones) · buyer's goods-in weight | Recycled fibre used per receipt · virgin cotton kg · yarn out · blend sheet, mixing log |
| **Fabric mill** (GRS) | GRS scope certificate · subcontractors' certificates (e.g. dyeing) | TC · purchase order · invoice · packing list · buyer's goods-in weight | Yarn used per receipt · fabric out · knitting plan, dye batch cards |
| **Garment manufacturer** (GRS) | GRS scope certificate | **To the EU buyer:** TC from its certification body · invoice · packing list · bill of lading | Fabric used per receipt · pieces · fabric per piece from the marker · garment weight · bill of materials · cutting report |
| **EU buyer** | — | Receives the claim, the final TC and the evidence pack | — |

Rules applied to all of it: quantities agree within ±2%, recycled % within ±0.5 points, documents dated within 45 days of shipment, names match the registered company, the scope certificate is valid on the shipment date and covers the product.

## Garment manufacturer (the customer)

1. **Invite your fabric mill.** *Suppliers → Invite supplier.* Each supplier then invites the next one up: mill → spinner → recycler → waste trader. An invitation link creates their account.
2. **Watch the chain fill in.** *Material flow* shows kg moving live; *Chain* shows every handoff for a claim with its checks.
3. **Raise gaps.** A failing check shows a *Raise gap* button. The supplier gets the request in its portal. The gap closes by itself when the data is fixed.
4. **Record your own production.** *Correct production record* on your cutting run: pieces, fabric per piece, garment weight.
5. **Release the claim.** The overview shows *Claim held* until every check back to the waste passes, then *Ready to release*.
6. **Prove it.** *Ledger → Check the chain* shows the history is intact.

## Waste trader (phone, works offline)

1. Tap **New batch** when you bag waste for sale.
2. **Batch:** weight, bags, weighbridge slip number, photo of the slip.
3. **Sources:** one card per factory: name, kg, collection date, waste type. The total must match the batch weight.
4. **Sort:** colour group, fibre, tick "no elastane" and "no prints".
5. **Sign** with your finger, or photograph the signed paper form.

No signal? Keep going. The top of the screen shows *Offline · n saved*; everything is sent when the phone reconnects.

## Recycler

1. **Goods-in queue** lists batches your traders have declared to you.
2. **Record goods-in:** date, weight at your gate, gate slip, moisture, your PO, the trader's invoice or cash memo, and tick the countersignature box if the delivery matches the declaration.
3. **Countersign** later if the declaration was signed after delivery.
4. **Record production:** waste used per goods-in, fibre out, sorting rejects.
5. **Record shipment** to the spinner with TC and documents.

## Spinner and fabric mill

1. **My tasks:** requests from your buyer and anything failing.
2. **Record production:** inputs per receipt (plus virgin cotton at the spinner), output, production records.
3. **Record shipment:** TC number and date, PO, invoice, packing list (net weight).
4. Open a shipment to correct any document; red cells in the cross-check show what disagrees.

## Everyone

- **Who sees what:** your own records and your direct buyer and seller. The manufacturer sees the chain. Nobody sees prices.
- **Connection status** is shown at the top: *Live*, *Sending…* or *Offline · n saved*. Tap it to see waiting changes, and any the server did not accept.
