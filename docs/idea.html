Threadback

Proof of recycled content, from cutting waste to garment.

A shared ledger where every company in the chain records what it handled, and every handoff is cross-checked automatically.

The problem

The evidence breaks at the first step.

Brands receive certificates from the garment factory. Nobody sees whether the waste at the start of the chain was ever real.

  1. Waste traderCollects cutting waste from factoriesno certificate
  2. RecyclerShreds it into fibreGRS · TC
  3. SpinnerBlends 40% recycled into yarnGRS · TC
  4. Fabric millKnits and dyesGRS · TC
  5. Garment makerCuts and sewsGRS · TC
  6. EU brandMakes the claimneeds proof

The idea

One ledger answers three questions for every claim.

OriginWhere did the material come from? Every kilogram traced to a named factory, signed by the trader, confirmed by the recycler.
DocumentationWho handled it? At every handoff, the documents must tell the same story.
Mass balanceDo the kilograms add up? Nobody can ship more recycled content than they received.

Cross-checking

Every handoff: five sources, one story.

Each document is read against the transaction certificate. Any value outside tolerance stops the claim.

The result

A claim is released only when the whole chain agrees.

All checks passThe claim is released with an evidence pack: every certificate, document, declaration and check, ready for the brand and the auditor.
Any check failsA request goes to the company that owns the data. It closes by itself once the data is corrected.
